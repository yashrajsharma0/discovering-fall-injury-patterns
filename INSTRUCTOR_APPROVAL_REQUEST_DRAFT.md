**Subject:** CA-1 project - request for approval of NEISS dataset substitution

Dear Sir/Ma'am,

Our CA-1 allocation refers to the DrivenData competition “Unsupervised Wisdom: Explore Medical Narratives on Older Adult Falls” (Competition 217). We have not used the competition-specific dataset in our current implementation. Instead, our working analysis uses prepared CPSC NEISS public-use records for 2019-2022, restricted to adults aged 65+ and screened using a broad set of fall-related keywords.

We understand that these rows are candidates rather than confirmed falls, and that NEISS covers consumer-product-related emergency-department injury surveillance rather than every older-adult fall. Accordingly, our current report describes this as an exploratory dataset substitution and does not present candidate counts or cluster shares as the prevalence of all falls.

Could you please confirm in writing whether this NEISS substitution is acceptable for the CA-1 project, or advise us to use a different source or narrow the scope? We will follow your guidance and update the report and implementation accordingly.
